What changed, and one thing that needs deciding

Common name is the headline; the Latin sits under it, small. The taxonomic key and the candidate list still exist — behind “Where this sits” and “Which one might it be?” — so nothing honest was removed, it just stopped leading.

Confidence is a ring with a colour, and the sentence carries the meaning. Green means sure, amber means the app stopped short on purpose.

Not green-chromed. Arter and Seek are both dark green; this stays warm, which is the one cheap way to not look like either.

The differentiator is narrower than BUILD.md thinks. Seek already falls back: “WE BELIEVE THIS IS A MEMBER OF THE FAMILY — Katydids. But Seek couldn’t identify the exact species.” What Seek does not do is tell you how sure, show what it was choosing between, or let you move the bar. That is what is actually left — and it is a thinner claim than “nobody else does this”.
