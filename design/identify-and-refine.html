What this commits to

A record hangs on the node it was determined at, and every ancestor shows it. Browsing Carabidae shows both the species record and the genus one. That is the tree doing the work — no "unidentified" bucket off to one side, which is where other apps put things and where users never look again.

Your determination is stored as yours. When you break the tie between two bush-crickets, the record says you decided, not the model. Overwriting the model's answer with a user tap and then reporting it as an identification would be the same overclaim in a new place.

The reference photo is not decoration. It is there to be compared against, so each candidate carries the one feature that actually separates it — wing length, the stripe. A thumbnail with no field mark is just reassurance.

Open: reference photos come from iNaturalist under CC licences, so each needs its photographer credited in-app. §8 already flags attribution as a real obligation — this is the screen where it bites, and it is worth deciding now whether credits sit under the photo or on a per-species page.
